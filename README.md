
# 🧬 Hi, I'm a microbiome researcher!

🔬 Biomedical scientist with a PhD in Biotechnology, interested in understanding how microbial communities interact with the human host and influence health and disease.

My research sits at the intersection of **experimental microbiology, molecular biology and bioinformatics**, combining wet-lab and computational approaches to explore the microbiome.
